**Status:** Deferred, non-V1 proposals only

**Purpose:** Preserve potentially useful ideas without expanding the approved V1 scope.

---

# 1. Authority Rule

This document is a backlog, not a product specification.

Adding an item here does not:

* approve it for implementation;
* add it to V1;
* authorize new infrastructure;
* change a locked product or engineering decision.

An item may enter V1 only through an explicit amendment recorded in
`DECISIONS.md`. Otherwise it may be considered after V1 at a deliberate product
checkpoint.

---

# 2. Entry Format

Each future proposal should record:

```text
Proposal:
User problem:
Reason deferred:
Earliest reconsideration point:
Related decision or specification:
```

Avoid detailed implementation planning until the proposal is approved.
